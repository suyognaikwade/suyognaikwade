### 👨‍💻 Executive Overview

> **"Disciplined engineering meets human-centric innovation."**

I am a Technology Leader, Full-Stack Architect, and Entrepreneur with a passion for designing scalable enterprise platforms, automating mission-critical infrastructure, and pioneering applied AI solutions. As the Founder and Managing Director of **[E-Sutra Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sutraatech)**, I drive digital transformation across MSMEs, government agencies, PSUs, and global enterprises.

* 🏢 **Executive Leadership**: Leading engineering culture, product innovation, and cross-functional teams delivering high-concurrency systems.
* 🏆 **Industry Recognition**: Honored as **Customer Success Leader of the Year (Full-Stack IT) - 2025** by *C-Suite Insider*.
* 🏛️ **Architecture & Systems**: Advocate of Feature-Sliced Design (FSD), decoupled Microservices, strict type safety, and zero-trust CI/CD delivery.
* 🤖 **Applied AI & Autonomy**: Building agentic workflows, local LLM architectures (LM Studio / Ollama), and intelligent development pipelines.
* 🚀 **End-to-End Delivery**: Bridging product strategy from raw concept to enterprise-scale deployment.
